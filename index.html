Important accuracy note

The PDF parser extracts text and then matches common trade-bill patterns. Always compare the extracted figures with the original PDF. Some brokers put tax totals at bill level rather than per stock, so the app distributes those shared charges by turnover; that distribution is not an official broker calculation. This version does not calculate capital-gains tax or income tax.

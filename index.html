Calculation rules & privacy

STT is calculated by transaction type where recognized (J intraday: 0.025% on sell only; D delivery: 0.1% on both buy and sell). Stamp duty is allocated only to buy rows. Transaction and SEBI charges are allocated by turnover; bill-level GST is allocated by eligible brokerage + transaction + SEBI service-charge base. Round-off is an estimated turnover-based allocation. These per-trade splits are estimates because the broker reports some charges only at bill level; compare totals with the source contract note. This tool does not calculate capital-gains tax or income tax. PDF text is processed locally in your browser.
